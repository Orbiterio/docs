Leverage Loop suggestions identify who to introduce to a selected contact and explain why the two people would benefit from connecting.

The existing example introduces **Ethan Jacks to Katelyn**. Its WHY section contains a headline and supporting paragraphs; its actions offer a double opt-in warm introduction and an email to Ethan.

[View the LEVERAGE LOOP sample payload](/guides/open-work/suggestion-delivery-and-tables/sample-suggestion-data#leverage-loop).
