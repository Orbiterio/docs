Leverage Loop suggestions identify who to introduce to a selected contact and explain why the two people would benefit from connecting.

The existing example introduces **Ethan Jacks to Katelyn**. Its WHY section contains a headline and supporting paragraphs; its actions offer a double opt-in warm introduction and an email to Ethan.

[View the LEVERAGE LOOP sample payload](/guides/open-work/suggestion-delivery-and-tables/sample-suggestion-data#leverage-loop).

## Design reference

[Open the local Leverage Loop prototype](http://localhost:3003/).

The prototype uses the supplied Match Card content with the Midnight palette and shared styles from [Outcome UI](/guides/open-work/suggestion-delivery-and-tables/outcome-ui). Ethan’s match opens by default, with the headline and four paragraphs in WHY. Rebekah and Bilawal remain separate collapsed match bars.

The main action shows **Double Opt-In Warm Introduction**. Its chevron reveals email and SMS alternatives. **Make Intro** opens editable drafts for asking Katelyn, asking Ethan, and introducing them after both agree. Drafts can be copied; the prototype does not send messages. **Edit content** enables temporary inline edits that reset on refresh.

Run `npm run dev:leverage` from `design/suggestion-delivery` to start port 3003. The editable implementation is in `LeverageLoop.jsx`, its content is in `leverage-data.js`, and its shared styling is in `SuggestionStyles.jsx` and `palettes.js`.
