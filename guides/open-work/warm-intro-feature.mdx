Warm Intro turns a Leverage Loop suggestion into a reviewed, automated double opt-in introduction. The sender starts from two known contacts and a natural-language WHY, reviews the three messages, and chooses who is asked first. Orbiter asks each person in sequence and introduces them only after both agree.

**Design baseline established; implementation planning underway.** Every introduction retains its WHY, sender intent, approved communication, consent decisions, and delivery history.
