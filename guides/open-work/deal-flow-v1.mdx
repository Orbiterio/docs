Deal Flow v1 is the product surface for flows: sets of stages that deals move through toward an outcome. A founder raising a round, an investor sourcing companies, and a team selling, partnering or hiring each run a flow. Every flow starts from a Deal Flow Template or from scratch. Orbiter ships two templates, Funding Round Raise and Enterprise Sale, and users can save their own.

**Planning only.** The work runs in this order:

1. Workshop the feature requirements.
2. Check whether the built tables and APIs need adjustments or additions.
3. Design the UI.
4. Write the build plan.
