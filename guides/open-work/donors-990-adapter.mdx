## Notes for Dennis

Two Go features in `orbiter-universe`, both on branch `ORBIT-1864/donor-enrichment` (tip `d73abf8`, 46 commits ahead of `main`, no PR yet, nothing deployed). Every flag defaults to `false`, so merging changes nothing until the dev env is flipped.

### What it does

**`internal/features/filings` — the IRS 990 adapter.** Given a nonprofit, it pulls the org's last four years of Form 990 filings (ProPublica for the inventory, IRS TEOS XML for the filing itself), stores the XML in GCS, and parses two things deterministically: the **board and officer roster** (Part VII) and the **grant lines** (Schedule I on a 990, Part XV on a 990-PF). The org is identified by EIN. People on the roster are only linked to a Person node when there is real corroboration (already affiliated with the org, a prior filing, or a LinkedIn profile plus an LLM adjudication at ≥ 0.85); a bare name never becomes a person.

**`internal/features/donors` — the Donors lane.** It never touches the IRS. It reads the grant lines the adapter parsed (through `filingspub.GrantReader`), matches each recipient to a School we track, and keeps a gift ledger per (foundation, School) pair. Phase 1 recipients are Stanford, NYU and Harvard, matched by filed EIN or a reviewed alias list, and only when the School is in `DONORS_RECIPIENT_ALLOWLIST`.

### Where it plugs into the waterfalls

One place: **company waterfall step 16, `filings_request`** (`internal/features/enrichment/company/steps_filings.go`). After `node_sync`, if the LLM classified the company as `nonprofit` or `educational` (or `is_school` is set) and it is not `government`, the step hands `{OrgEntityID, Name, Domain}` to the adapter. The adapter finds the EIN via ProPublica, attaches it to the entity the graph already holds, and queues it. A scheduler tick (`filings.queue_drain_requested`, every 5 min once enabled) drains the queue. The person waterfall is untouched.

Donors has no waterfall hook. Its `feed_scan` job runs on every Donors drain tick and turns each newly parsed filing into a `filing_replay` job automatically; a `grantor_request` job (an EIN) is how you ask for a foundation that is not yet in Universe.

### What we get: edges

| Edge | Direction | Weight | Written when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `BOARD_MEMBER_OF` | Person → Company:Organization | 8 | Roster row flagged trustee/director, person resolved, `board_member_of` fact chosen, both nodes exist, `FILINGS_990_EDGE_WRITES_ENABLED=true` |
| `OFFICER_OF` | Person → Company:Organization | 14 officer / 20 key employee | Same, for officer or key-employee rows |
| `DONOR_TO` | Company (990-PF foundation) → Company:School | 30; 40 if > 200 grants in the window | Recipient allowlisted, `donor_to` fact chosen, both nodes exist, `DONORS_EDGE_WRITES_ENABLED=true` |

Every edge carries `title_as_filed` / `gift_count`, `total_usd_known`, `valid_from` / `valid_to` or `first_gift_year` / `last_gift_year`, `filing_refs` or `mention_ref`, `source_urls`, `confidence`, `source: irs_990` and a templated description. Tenure and totals are recomputed from every contributing filing, so re-running changes nothing.

**When.** Rows land on the first drain; the graph edge lands on the next edge sweep (`filings.edge_sweep_requested` / `POST /v1/admin/donors/edge-sweep`) after the gate is open. For a school that enters through the company waterfall that is minutes after its first enrichment.

**Not written, on purpose:** people from bare names; `DONOR_TO` from a plain 990 or 990-EZ (only 990-PF filers are classified as foundations today); trustee-derived person → School `DONOR_TO` (the routes are stored relationally, projection is off); anything while a gate is closed.

### Smoke test 1: add Ford Foundation by domain

<Card icon="globe">

```
fordfoundation.org
```

**Ford Foundation** — EIN `13-1684331`, files a 990-PF, not in Universe today. Add it as a company through the normal enrichment flow; nothing else to call.

</Card>

**What happens.** Preflight classifies it `nonprofit` → step 16 hands `{entity, "Ford Foundation", fordfoundation.org}` to the adapter → ProPublica returns one exact-name hit with filings → EIN `131684331` is attached to the entity → the org is queued → the next 990 drain tick pulls four 990-PF filings (TY2020, 2022–2024; the IRS has no TY2021 XML for Ford) and parses 14,939 grant lines and 24–26 roster rows per filing → the next Donors tick's `feed_scan` turns the four filings into `filing_replay` jobs → gifts to the allowlisted Schools → the next edge sweep writes the graph.

**What to see** (once the gates are open and `DONORS_RECIPIENT_ALLOWLIST` holds the Stanford, NYU and Harvard entity ids):

```cypher
MATCH (f:Company {ein: '131684331'})-[r:DONOR_TO]->(s:School)
RETURN s.name, r.weight, r.gift_count, r.total_usd_known, r.first_gift_year, r.last_gift_year
```

| School | `gift_count` | `total_usd_known` | `weight` |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Stanford | 15 | 2,719,000 | 40 |
| NYU | 28 | 4,866,970 | 40 |
| Harvard | 37 | 9,139,545 | 40 |

Weight is 40 because Ford has more than 200 grants in the window. Ford's trustees who resolved also appear as `(:Person)-[:BOARD_MEMBER_OF]->(f)`. Adding `fordfoundation.org` a second time changes nothing. Detailed pass criteria: [990 Adapter Smoke Tests](/guides/open-work/roadmap/990-fec-form-d/phase-1-990-smoke-tests) (ST-3 is Ford).

### Smoke test 2: add a school that is not in Universe yet

This exercises what Ford does not: a recipient that arrives *after* its grants are already loaded. One constraint shapes it — **990-PF grant lines carry no recipient EIN** (0 of Ford's 14,939 do), so a recipient is matched by a reviewed alias, and today only Stanford, NYU and Harvard have one. Ford's filings already hold gifts to these schools, none of which are in the graph:

| Domain to add | School | Ford grants | Total | Filed name to alias |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `georgetown.edu` | Georgetown University | 25 | \$8,790,000 | `GEORGETOWN UNIVERSITY` |
| `howard.edu` | Howard University | 9 | \$3,833,500 | `HOWARD UNIVERSITY` |
| `yale.edu` | Yale University | 16 | \$2,523,400 | `YALE UNIVERSITY` |
| `columbia.edu` | Columbia University | 13 | \$1,933,000 | `THE TRUSTEES OF COLUMBIA UNIVERSITY IN THE CITY OF NEW YORK` |
| `mit.edu` | MIT | 11 | \$1,195,250 | `MASSACHUSETTS INSTITUTE OF TECHNOLOGY` |

Georgetown is the largest and cleanest:

<Card icon="globe">

```
georgetown.edu
```

**Georgetown University** — EIN `53-0196603`. Add it as a company. As a nonprofit it also triggers its own 990, so its board lands as `BOARD_MEMBER_OF` at the same time.

</Card>

Then three one-time settings so Donors can recognise it:

1. Add a reviewed alias row to `internal/features/donors/data/recipient_aliases.json` — recipient `georgetown` keyed by EIN `530196603` / domain `georgetown.edu`, alias `georgetown university`, region `DC`, `decision: map`, `approved: true` (copy the Stanford row's shape) — and run `make seed ONLY=donor-recipient-aliases`.
2. Append Georgetown's entity id to `DONORS_RECIPIENT_ALLOWLIST` and restart the worker.
3. Queue a `recipient_sweep` job for the Georgetown entity (admin UI or job endpoint). The sweep re-gates the Ford mentions already sitting in `donation_mention` — zero IRS or ProPublica calls, no replay.

**What to see** after the next edge sweep:

```cypher
MATCH (f:Company {ein: '131684331'})-[r:DONOR_TO]->(s:School {domain: 'georgetown.edu'})
RETURN s.name, r.weight, r.gift_count, r.total_usd_known
```

One row: weight 40, 25 gifts, 8,790,000. If the sweep reports `no_matching_grants_in_loaded_corpus`, the alias did not seed — check that the Georgetown entity resolved by EIN or domain in the seeder log.

Any school in the table works the same way. A school whose filed name is a legal-entity name (Columbia, or Boston University's `TRUSTEES OF BOSTON UNIVERSITY`) is exactly what the alias file is for; do not add a substring rule.
